Aircon Cleaning

Aircon Cleaning in Makati

Makati's condo corridor — from Ayala Center and Salcedo Village to Rockwell, Poblacion, and Bel-Air — is almost entirely split-type and window-type aircon, run daily against the heat and humidity of a high-rise unit. That makes it the highest-volume aircon cleaning area we serve, and the logistics of a condo tower (gate passes, service elevators, building-admin coordination) are different enough from a house that this page exists on its own.

What's included

  • Filter removal, wash, and reinstallation for each split-type or window-type unit
  • Coil and blower-fan cleaning to clear dust and mold buildup that blocks airflow
  • Drainage-line flush, to stop the ceiling-stain leaks common in older condo units
  • Exterior casing wipe-down and vent-cover cleaning
  • Post-clean cooling and drainage check before the crew leaves

Booking in a Makati condo tower

  • Gate pass and advance notice: most Makati towers require the crew's names and a scheduled time slot registered with building admin before entry — plan your booking a few days ahead of your preferred date.
  • Service elevator and core-zone access: crews use the service elevator and loading dock where the building designates one, keeping common areas clear for other residents.
  • No zone surcharge: Makati sits in Salára's core coverage zone, so there is no cross-zone travel surcharge for bookings here.

How pricing works

Aircon cleaning is priced by property type (studio, 1BR, 2BR, and up) and service tier — a routine check-up clean, or a deep-clean tier for units with heavier buildup or a first-time service. Larger units typically carry more aircon heads, which is why property size is the pricing driver; tell us your unit count and types (split-type, window-type) when requesting a quote so the total is exact.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to register the crew with my building's admin office?
Most Makati condo buildings require this. We share the crew's details ahead of the visit so you can register them with admin or security in advance — check your building's specific process, since it varies by tower.
Can the crew clean units in towers without direct owner access, like a unit I rent out?
Yes — coordinate entry through the tenant, building admin, or a lockbox, the same options offered on our move-out cleaning page.
Is aircon cleaning here priced differently from other Metro Manila areas?
No — pricing follows the same property-type and tier structure as every other core-zone location, with no cross-zone surcharge since Makati is core coverage.
