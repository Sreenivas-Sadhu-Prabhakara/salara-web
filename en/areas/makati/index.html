Cleaning in Makati

Cleaning built for Ayala CBD condo towers and Makati homes.

Get a Quote

Makati's central business district holds Metro Manila's densest concentration of high-rise condo towers — the corridor along Ayala Avenue and Paseo de Roxas, and the residential enclaves of Salcedo Village and Legazpi Village, where visitor registration and an elevator booking are part of every service visit, not an exception.

We work within that routine as a matter of course: valid government ID for our crew, advance notice to your building admin, and a confirmed elevator slot lined up before we arrive, so your appointment doesn't stall at the guard desk. For move-out cleaning ahead of a Makati condo turnover inspection, we time the visit around your gate pass and deposit return.

Buildings we serve

We regularly clean units in the high-rise towers along Ayala Avenue and Paseo de Roxas, and in the residential enclaves of Salcedo Village, Legazpi Village, and Bel-Air — from studio units for young professionals to full houses in the low-rise Bel-Air blocks.

Zone & pricing

Makati sits in our core coverage zone, so jobs here are priced at our standard rate with no travel surcharge. Bookings in outer-zone cities elsewhere in Metro Manila may carry a small surcharge — ask when you request a quote.

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle building registration and elevator booking in Makati condo towers?
Yes. Tell us your building name and unit details when you book, and we coordinate the standard visitor registration and elevator reservation most Ayala Avenue and Salcedo Village towers require. If your building needs more than 24 hours' notice, flag it and we'll plan the date around it.
Can you clean before a move-out inspection in a Makati condo?
Yes — our move-out cleaning is timed around your gate pass appointment and deposit return, and cleaned to the standard most Makati building admins expect at turnover.
Do you clean houses in Bel-Air and Forbes Park, or only condo units?
Both. We clean condo units of any size as well as full houses in Makati's low-rise residential villages.
