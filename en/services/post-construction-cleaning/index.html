What's included

  • Fine construction dust and debris removal from all surfaces
  • Paint speck and sticker/label removal from tiles, glass, and fixtures
  • Window, frame, and track detailing
  • Air vent and light-fixture dust removal
  • Floor-by-floor sweep, vacuum, and mop, room by room

How pricing works

Unlike the other services, post-construction cleaning is priced per square meter rather than by property type — dust density after a renovation or fit-out does not track unit-type tiers the way routine cleaning does. Provide your unit's floor area in square meters when requesting a quote.

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from a deep clean?
Deep cleaning targets buildup in an already-lived-in unit. Post-construction cleaning is a first clean for fine dust, paint specks, and debris left after renovation, fit-out, or a developer turnover — usually a heavier and more time-intensive job.
How soon after handover should this be booked?
As soon as the contractors are done and the unit is accessible. Booking before you move in furniture avoids re-contaminating cleaned surfaces with more dust.
Do you haul away bulky construction debris?
The service covers cleaning of fine dust and residue on surfaces, not hauling of bulky construction waste. Confirm scope for your specific unit condition when requesting a quote.
