About Salára

Named for light, built for calm

Salára is a new residential cleaning brand for Metro Manila, launching with a premium, unhurried standard of service.

Where the name comes from

Salára is a coined name built from the Kapampangan word sála — light as natural radiance, brightness, clarity — with an invented ending. It has no dictionary meaning in any language, on purpose: it is ours to define, and we're defining it as a calm, premium standard for a category that, in Metro Manila, has mostly been run through a Facebook page and a Viber number.

The brand draws on Pampanga's giant-lantern (parul) tradition — a mark of concentric, radiating light rather than a festive filled star — paired with a warm, restrained palette: sála amber, deep indigo, Capiz off-white, and Betis umber, a nod to the master woodcarvers of Guagua. The goal is a brand that reads as hospitality-grade and unhurried, not a hard sell.

Why Salára

  • DTI/SEC-registered business, with BIR official receipts for every job.
  • NBI-cleared, trained cleaning staff.
  • Eco-friendly products that are safe for children and pets.

Insurance & bonding

We do not carry insurance or bonding coverage at this time. Rather than imply a protection we don't yet have, we're stating it plainly — this is a known gap, and this section is reserved to be filled in once a policy is in place.

How booking works today

Salára doesn't have self-serve online booking yet. Right now, request a quote through our online form and we'll get back to you by email (or your preferred contact method) to confirm the details and a date. Full online booking — real-time availability, instant pricing, no back-and-forth — is coming soon; the quote form is the fastest way to reach us in the meantime.
